# PROJECT OBJECTIVE

JanVoice AI is a civic engagement platform where citizens can:

- Create an account and verify their identity.
- Browse and join public campaigns (education, infrastructure, healthcare, environment, public services, etc.).
- Sign digital petitions.
- Submit structured feedback.
- Upload supporting documents and evidence.
- Record or upload short video testimonies.
- Participate in public opinion polls.
- Generate AI-assisted emails and letters based on their own input.
- Review and approve emails before sending them using their own email client or another transparent workflow.
- Track campaign progress.
- View public analytics and dashboards.
- Receive campaign updates.
- Participate in moderated community discussions.

The platform must be **generic**, not dedicated to any one political issue or person.
